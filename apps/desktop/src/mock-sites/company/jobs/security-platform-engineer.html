About the team

Platform Security & Trust builds the paved road that every Meridian engineering team ships on. We own the internal supply-chain security tooling, the policy engine that gates production deploys, and the secrets platform used by roughly 900 engineers.

This is a builder role, not a reviewer role. You will spend most of your week writing Go and Python services, not filling in spreadsheets.

What you'll do

  • Design and operate the signing and provenance pipeline for all internal artifacts.
  • Extend our policy-as-code layer so security requirements ship as reviewable code.
  • Build self-service remediation tooling that closes vulnerabilities without tickets.
  • Partner with infrastructure teams on identity, secrets and workload attestation.
  • Own the developer experience of security controls end to end.

Required qualifications

  • 5+ years building production software in a systems or platform context.
  • Hands-on experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform or equivalent).
  • Practical CI/CD pipeline engineering, including release gating.
  • Experience remediating vulnerabilities in a large dependency surface.
  • Strong Python or Go.

Preferred qualifications

  • Exposure to supply-chain security frameworks (SLSA, in-toto, Sigstore).
  • Experience shipping developer tooling used by other engineers.
  • Familiarity with container orchestration in a production environment.
  • Interest in applying LLM-based automation to security workflows.

Compensation and location

Base range $185,000 – $225,000 plus equity. Fully remote within the United States. No relocation required. This role does not require a security clearance.
